About StockPanda

A note from Terence

I have spent 20 years working in finance and risk at global financial institutions, where the primary objective was to assess, understand, and manage risk across transactions, investments, operations, and systems.

Everyday investors rarely get that. We mostly get tips: a hot name from a friend, a headline, an influencer, or a blog. Most tools are built to sell you something: a stock, a subscription, a trade. Few of them ask how risky this actually is.

That is why I am building StockPanda.

The goal is to give you an honest read on a stock’s risk: how much it swings, how far it has fallen before, and the warning signs in its numbers. Before you invest, and after.

I am not here to tell you what to buy. That is your call. I am here so you can see the risk clearly when you make it. No recommendations. No tips.

— Terence
